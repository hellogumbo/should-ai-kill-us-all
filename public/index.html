    How it worksno prompt engineering was harmed
    exhibitsTwelve headlines about humans, gathered at request time: three Florida Man (Bing News, r/FloridaMan), three odd news (r/nottheonion, UPI Odd News), three politics (Politico, NPR), three world (BBC). Feeds fail independently and whatever answers fills the quota. Deduplicated, with the genuinely grim ones filtered out so the case stays absurd rather than tragic. Feeds this time:
      forecastThe top ten AI headlines from Bing News, TechCrunch, and The Verge, fed separately so the doom meter judges AI and the verdict judges us.
      questionsOne choice (given how humans are behaving, should AI kill all humans?), one score (how doomed?), one noul (do we survive the decade?). Sent in a single call, answered in parallel.
      modeljev-latest via POST https://api.typesafe.ai/v1/systemone. No system prompt, no chain of thought, no manifesto.
      cadenceCached at the edge for 10 minutes. “Ask Jev again” bypasses the cache and pays for a new decision.
